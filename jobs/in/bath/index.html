Working in Bath

Bath is a World Heritage city with a large visitor economy, so hotels, restaurants, cafés and shops hire all year and especially at weekends and in the run-up to Christmas. Bath Spa station is a few minutes from the centre, and trains run to Keynsham and Bristol in around 10 to 15 minutes.

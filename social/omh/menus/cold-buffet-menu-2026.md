## General Information

Thank you for choosing us as a venue to host your event. Please read the information below that applies to this menu. If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.

**Menu**
We require the final number that we are catering for, along with any allergies or dietary requirements, 7 days prior to the booking.
Should you require any allergen or dietary information, please contact the hotel directly and we can assist with your menu choices.

**Drinks**
Please make us aware if you will be running a bar tab for your guests. We will require a card to be held behind the bar to run a tab.
Wine lists/bottle prices can be provided on request. Please note drinks prices can fluctuate.

**Deposit & Payment**
This menu requires a non-refundable deposit of £10 per head to secure the date. Once a provisional booking has been made, we will give you a deadline for a date that a deposit must be paid by, or the booking will be cancelled and offered to another group.
Once we know the final number that we are catering for, we will contact you to make the final payment. Should any of your guests cancel the food can only be refunded providing no preparation has already taken place.

**Exclusivity**
Please note exclusivity of the function room can only be offered to groups of 25 and over. If you would like exclusivity of the function room with less than this number, the remaining charges would be added to your bill. Exclusivity of the restaurant can be offered to groups of 20 and over.

**Decorations**
Only bio-degradable confetti is permitted on-site. We DO NOT allow any loose ballons because this is a trip hazard to staff, or anything stuck to the walls because it ruins the paint. Photos/tied balloons can be hung from the window frames and mantelpieces. Failure to comply may lead to damage charges. Thank you for your cooperation.

*We look forward to hosting your event* 😊

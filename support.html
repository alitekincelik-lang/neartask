Common questions

  • I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and we will email you a link to set a new one.
  • I am not getting notifications. Open Profile, then Notifications, and check they are turned on. Also check that notifications are allowed for NearTask in your phone settings.
  • I want to report a listing or a person. Use Report listing on the listing, or report or block the person from their profile. Our team reviews every report.
  • I want to delete my account. Open Profile and choose Delete account. This removes your profile, listings, offers, messages and photos. More detail is on the Delete your account page.
  • Does NearTask cost anything? No. It is free to post, there is no commission, and you agree pay directly with each other.

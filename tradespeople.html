How it works

  • Choose your trades and postcode. When someone near you posts a request in your trade, you get an alert.
  • Send a quote with your price and when you are free. The customer sees quotes side by side.
  • Agree and get it done. Chat in the app, do the job and collect your review.
  • Offer fixed-price services too, like "Boiler service, £80", that customers book in one tap.

Show your credentials

Add your Gas Safe licence number, your electrical registration (NICEIC, NAPIT, ELECSA or Stroma) and the end date of your public liability insurance. They appear on your profile and your quotes, and customers can check them on the official registers. Our team checks them too and adds a Checked by NearTask mark.

Gas work in the UK must be done by a Gas Safe registered engineer. On NearTask, only engineers with a Gas Safe number can quote for requests marked as gas work. Check any engineer on the Gas Safe Register.
